# Privacy Policy for Bumblebee Bus

**Effective Date:** August 23, 2025

Bumblebee Bus is a standalone Apple Watch app that displays real-time public transit arrival times for the 30 Stockton bus in San Francisco. The app is intended for personal use and does not collect or share any personal data.

## Information Collection
Bumblebee Bus does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal information about users. The app does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies.

## Data Usage
The app retrieves public transit data from the 511.org API (Metropolitan Transportation Commission). All information displayed in the app is publicly available and does not include any personally identifiable information.

## Children’s Privacy
Bumblebee Bus is safe for children to use. It does not contain advertising, in-app purchases, or links to external websites. The app complies with Apple’s requirements for apps that may be used by children under the age of 13.
